## Read the protocol as a contract

Protocol analysis begins with semantics: message ordering, required fields, error states, replay behavior and assumptions about peers.

## Failure modes

A protocol can be syntactically valid while semantically unsafe. Ambiguous state transitions and inconsistent validation are often more interesting than malformed packets.

## Research method

Capture the expected state machine, construct synthetic fixtures, then vary one assumption at a time. The output should be a reproducible observation, not a dramatic exploit narrative.
