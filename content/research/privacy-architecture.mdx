## Minimize first

Privacy architecture starts by asking whether a piece of data needs to exist at all. If it does, its purpose, lifetime and access path should be explicit.

## Useful controls

- collect only what the feature needs
- prefer ephemeral state for temporary workflows
- separate product telemetry from security evidence
- make retention visible to operators

## Interface matters

A privacy control that is technically present but difficult to understand is easy to bypass accidentally. Good privacy UX makes collection and sharing boundaries legible without turning every screen into a warning dialog.
