## Observability before interpretation

Reverse-engineering becomes more reliable when acquisition, normalization and interpretation are separate stages.

A repeatable workflow records the original image, calculates a deterministic fingerprint, identifies known regions and only then performs deeper inspection.

## Boot-flow map

```text
reset → firmware volume → boot manager → loader → operating environment
```

Unexpected transitions should be treated as evidence to investigate, not proof of compromise. The fictional archive keeps that distinction explicit.
