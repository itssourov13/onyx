## Machine actors need boundaries

An agent that can act across multiple services needs more than a name. It needs scoped authority, expiration and an audit trail that survives delegation.

## Capability over convenience

A broad credential is easy to integrate but difficult to reason about. Narrow capabilities make the allowed action explicit and provide a smaller blast radius when an integration changes.

## Auditability

An event record should answer who acted, under which capability, on what resource, with what outcome and whether another principal delegated the action.

## Open questions

The project treats machine identity as a research topic rather than an implemented authentication system. Future experiments can add synthetic principals without introducing real credentials.
