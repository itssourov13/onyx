## Signal

Machine identity becomes difficult when one credential spans several services with different policy requirements.

## Evidence pattern

Separate principal identity, capability scope, delegation and expiry in both configuration and audit records.

## Interpretation

The record is synthetic and deliberately contains no credentials or real service identifiers.
