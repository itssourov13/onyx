## Signal

Privacy controls work better when the interface explains what is collected, why it exists and how long it persists.

## Evidence pattern

Review default persistence, sharing prompts, telemetry labels and destructive/reset controls.

## Interpretation

The signal is editorial, not a claim about any external product.
