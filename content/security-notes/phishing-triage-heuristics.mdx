## Local triage

A suspicious URL can often be reviewed without fetching it. Inspect scheme, host structure, punycode, embedded credentials, unusual ports and excessive subdomain depth.

## Heuristics are not proof

A single signal can be benign. Treat the result as a triage aid and escalate for deeper analysis when appropriate.

## Safe workflow

```text
paste URL → parse locally → inspect signals → record decision
```

The Onyx lab deliberately does not crawl, redirect to, submit to or fetch the supplied URL.
